# Selection Page

## How to choose

- Robotics — Design, build, and program machines that interact with the real world — from robotic arms to autonomous drones.

- AI & Machine Learning — Dive into algorithms that learn from data, recognize patterns, and power everything from chatbots to self-driving cars.

- Biotechnology & Bioinformatics — Merge biology and computing to study genes, proteins, and diseases — and help shape the future of medicine.

- Astronomy — Study stars, planets, and galaxies to understand our place in the universe and what lies beyond it.
Nuclear Science — Explore atomic energy, radiation, and nuclear reactions — the science behind power plants and medical imaging.
